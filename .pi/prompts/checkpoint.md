description: Capture a Command Protocol checkpoint
argument-hint: "[scope]"
---
Use the `command-protocol` skill. Interpret this as `CHECKPOINT $ARGUMENTS` under `AGENTS.md`. Produce a concise evidence snapshot for the exact current state. Include: current phase; revision or other state identity; validated evidence; files changed; tests and actual results; blockers; remaining risks; authorization state; and next permitted transition. Mark unknown items as unknown rather than inventing evidence. A checkpoint grants no new authorization.
